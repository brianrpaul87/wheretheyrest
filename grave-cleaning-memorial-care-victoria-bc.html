Greater Victoria service area

Local first, then carefully wider.

Where They Rest is based in Greater Victoria and is now accepting local requests. That includes requests in Victoria and surrounding communities such as Saanich, Oak Bay, Esquimalt, View Royal, Colwood, Langford, and the Saanich Peninsula, subject to cemetery rules and confirmed service coverage.

We do not claim that every cemetery or every memorial can receive every service. Access, cleaning, flowers, decorations, provider requirements, and family authorization can differ from one cemetery to another.

What we do

Visits, photos, light tending, approved tributes, eligible surface cleaning, and family/cemetery coordination.

What we do not pretend to do

Structural repair, monument resetting, excavation, engineering assessment, and aggressive restoration remain work for the appropriate cemetery or monument professional.

Why families use remote memorial care

You can still care for a resting place when you live far away.

A family member may be buried in Victoria while children or grandchildren now live elsewhere in British Columbia, another province, the United States, or overseas. Health, mobility, work, cost, and distance can all make cemetery visits difficult.

Where They Rest is built around that problem. The service is not simply “make the stone cleaner.” It is to provide a dependable local visit, follow the applicable rules, complete only the work that was approved, and give the family a clear record afterward.

Victoria memorial care FAQ

Before you request a visit

Can you clean any headstone in Greater Victoria?

No. We first consider cemetery rules, memorial material, condition, age, inscriptions, coatings, and the requested scope. A memorial that is unstable, flaking, cracked, historically sensitive, or otherwise unsuitable for routine cleaning should be referred to the appropriate professional.

Can I arrange a visit if I live outside Victoria?

Yes. The service is specifically designed for families who cannot easily visit in person. You can submit a request from another province or country.

Will I receive photos?

Completed visits include dated photographs and concise care notes appropriate to the approved service.

Do you pressure wash gravestones?

Pressure washing is not part of standard Where They Rest memorial care. Cleaning methods must be appropriate to the stone and its condition and must comply with cemetery rules.

Can you place flowers for an anniversary or birthday?

Where cemetery rules permit the requested tribute, we can coordinate placement and provide a photo record. Flower or tribute costs are confirmed separately before purchase.
