Privacy Policy

We collect only the information reasonably needed to review requests, communicate with families and partners, protect the website, and provide approved memorial-care services.

Last updated September 11, 2026

Privacy contact: Where They Rest has designated the position of Privacy Officer to oversee privacy compliance. Questions, access requests, correction requests, or complaints can be sent to hello@wheretheyrest.ca or raised by phone at (778) 561-1007.

1. Who we are

Where They Rest Memorial Care is a Canadian memorial-care service based in Greater Victoria, British Columbia. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through wheretheyrest.ca and through related communications and service requests.

2. What we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number, and other contact information you choose to provide;
  • the cemetery city or community, province or territory, cemetery name, memorial-location details, and the type of service you are considering;
  • messages, correspondence, photographs, service instructions, and information needed to confirm that requested work is authorized and permitted;
  • records relating to quotes, approved services, scheduling, payments, completed visits, and care reports;
  • limited technical and security information needed to protect the website from abuse. Our public request endpoint uses an IP address to create a hashed rate-limit key and is designed not to retain the raw IP address in that rate-limit record; and
  • website usage information collected through Google Analytics, which may include browser or device information, pages viewed, referral information, approximate location, and identifiers or cookies used by Google Analytics.

Please do not send medical, financial, government-identification, password, or other highly sensitive information through the public request form unless we specifically ask for it through an appropriate process.

3. Why we collect and use information

We use personal information for purposes a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the circumstances, including to:

  • review and respond to inquiries;
  • confirm cemetery location, access requirements, local service coverage, and requested scope;
  • prepare quotes and arrange approved memorial-care services;
  • communicate with you, a cemetery operator, or a service provider when reasonably necessary to complete an authorized request;
  • create service records, photographs, care reports, and related business records;
  • protect the website, prevent spam or misuse, and troubleshoot technical issues; and
  • understand general website usage and improve our services and communications.

4. Consent and limiting collection

Where consent is required, we seek consent appropriate to the sensitivity of the information and the purpose for which it is collected, used, or disclosed. We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary for the identified purpose. You may withdraw consent subject to legal, contractual, or practical restrictions; withdrawal may affect our ability to provide a requested service.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose information only as reasonably necessary for the identified purpose or as permitted or required by law. This can include disclosure to:

  • hosting, email, analytics, payment, bookkeeping, or other service providers supporting our operations;
  • a cemetery operator when rules, permission, location confirmation, or access coordination require it;
  • a local memorial steward or qualified provider when information is needed to complete an approved service; and
  • legal, regulatory, law-enforcement, insurance, or professional advisers where disclosure is lawfully required or reasonably necessary.

Some technology providers, including Google, may process information outside Canada. Information processed in another jurisdiction may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

6. Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for legitimate legal, tax, insurance, dispute-resolution, or business-record requirements. Where applicable law requires information used to make a decision directly affecting an individual to be retained for a specified period, we follow that requirement.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No email system, website, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Access and correction

You may ask to access personal information about you that is in our custody or control, or ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate. Some legal exceptions may apply. Please contact the Privacy Officer using the information above.

8. Google Analytics and browser controls

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and improve the site. Google may use cookies or similar technologies as part of that service. You can limit cookies using your browser settings and can use Google-provided browser controls where available. Blocking analytics technologies should not prevent you from submitting a memorial-care request.

9. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, please contact our Privacy Officer first so we can investigate and respond. Private-sector organizations in British Columbia are generally subject to British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), which is overseen by the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The current version will be posted on this page with the revision date.
