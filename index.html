About Where They Rest

Care should not depend on living nearby.

Where They Rest is a Canadian memorial-care service based in Greater Victoria, British Columbia. We exist for families who care deeply about a resting place but cannot always be there in person.

Our role is straightforward: understand what the family wants, confirm what the cemetery permits, coordinate appropriate local care, and return a documented record of the visit.

We are intentionally building the service community by community rather than pretending to have coverage everywhere before it exists.

Cemetery rules come first

Access, permissions, provider requirements, decorations, and cleaning restrictions are checked before work is accepted.

Documented, not vague

Completed visits are supported by dated photographs and concise care notes so families can see what happened.

Clear service boundaries

Structural repair, monument resetting, excavation, and aggressive restoration are outside standard memorial care.

A quote before a commitment

The public request form is not a booking. Availability, scope, timing, and price are confirmed before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Respectful answers before you decide

Do you clean every type of memorial?

Not automatically. Memorial material, condition, age, inscriptions, coatings, and cemetery restrictions must be considered first. Unsafe or inappropriate cleaning methods will not be used.

Do you need cemetery permission?

Requirements differ by cemetery. Where permission, approved-provider status, or family authorization is required, it must be confirmed before service.

Can I request a visit on a specific date?

You can request a meaningful date, but timing depends on cemetery access, weather, local availability, and the agreed service window.

Will you repair a damaged or leaning headstone?

Not as part of standard memorial care. We can document visible concerns and, where possible, help identify the appropriate cemetery or qualified monument professional.

Can I request care if I live in another province or country?

Yes. You can submit a request from anywhere. Service is confirmed only after the cemetery location, access requirements, and suitable local coverage are verified.

What is included in a photo and condition update?

A typical update includes dated photographs of the memorial and surrounding area, a concise description of visible conditions, and notes about any concern that may require the cemetery or a qualified monument professional.
