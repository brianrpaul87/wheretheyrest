# Greater Victoria Partner Prospect Sheet

Last researched: 2026-09-11

## Operating rule

This is a research and draft-preparation list, **not an authorization-to-send list**.

For Canadian commercial email, the final sender must retain evidence supporting consent and must include identification/contact information and a working unsubscribe mechanism. Where the working theory is implied consent by conspicuous publication, re-check the source immediately before sending to confirm:

1. the address is still conspicuously published by the account holder or recipient;
2. there is no accompanying statement refusing unsolicited commercial electronic messages; and
3. the message is directly relevant to the recipient's business role, functions, or duties.

CRTC guidance:
- https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm
- https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm
- https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/internet/anti/reg.htm

**Status definitions**
- `DRAFT-READY — RECHECK BEFORE SEND`: current first-party publication located; role relevance appears strong. Still requires owner approval and final source/no-solicit recheck.
- `RELATIONSHIP / NON-SALES`: useful sector relationship but should not receive a hard commercial pitch.
- `FORM/PHONE ONLY`: current first-party site does not conspicuously publish a general email that we should use. Do not substitute an old directory address.
- `RESEARCH MORE`: evidence is incomplete or stale. Do not email yet.

## First-wave sequence

### Wave A — owner-approved email drafts
Prepare individualized drafts for prospects 1–5. Each draft should:
- be a one-to-one relationship inquiry, not a newsletter;
- reference a specific aspect of the recipient's actual work;
- explain WTR in one sentence;
- state that WTR follows cemetery/operator rules and does not perform structural monument restoration as standard care;
- ask one simple question about fit or policy;
- include Where They Rest identity, phone, website, mailing/business contact information as applicable, and an easy opt-out;
- have the exact source URL and re-check date recorded before send.

### Wave B — relationship, not sales
Prospect 6 should receive a respectful relationship/knowledge note only, after owner review.

### Wave C — contact-form / phone
Prospects 8–12 should not be fed to an autonomous cold-email sequence. Use current official forms/phone to identify the right person and obtain the appropriate contact route.

### Province-wide later test
Prospect 7 can be tested after local cemetery/industry conversations have sharpened the proposition.

## Marblism / Stan instructions for this sheet

1. Never change a `FORM/PHONE ONLY` row to email-ready using a third-party data broker alone.
2. Before any email draft is placed in the approval queue, revisit the exact first-party source URL and log the current date.
3. If the page says not to send unsolicited/commercial email, mark `DO NOT EMAIL`.
4. Keep outreach draft-only until the owner explicitly approves the message and recipient.
5. If a recipient opts out or asks not to be contacted, mark them suppressed immediately and do not contact them again through another employee/agent.
6. Do not infer or fabricate personal staff email addresses.
7. Do not claim WTR is approved by, partnered with, or recommended by any organization until a real agreement exists.

## Research notes

- Mortimer's and Meadowlark are not merely competitors; they are potentially useful specialist referral partners because WTR's standard scope intentionally excludes structural repairs, resetting, engraving, and aggressive restoration.
- The Old Cemeteries Society already offers very inexpensive historic grave lookup/photo services. WTR should not position itself against that volunteer heritage function. The commercial value proposition is ongoing family care, coordination, approved tending/tributes, and service reporting.
- Funeral-home outreach should be framed as a **post-burial family resource**, not an attempt to intrude into funeral arrangements.
- Cemetery-operator outreach should lead with **permission and compliance**, not with a request to be placed on a vendor/referral list.
