# Where They Rest — Marblism Launch Brief

Last updated: 2026-09-11

## Primary objective

Validate Where They Rest as a real paid memorial-care business in Greater Victoria before building a national network.

The first 90-day objective is not follower count or email volume. It is:

- 10+ paid customers who are not friends/family
- 3+ recurring-care customers
- 5+ genuine customer reviews/testimonials
- 10+ meaningful cemetery/referral-partner conversations
- 2 dependable local memorial-care providers in addition to owner-delivered coverage where appropriate
- measurable organic/inbound inquiries

## Brand position

Where They Rest is **remote memorial care**, not a monument-restoration company.

Core promise:

> A family can care for a resting place even when distance, health, mobility, work, or life makes visiting difficult.

Differentiators:

- cemetery-first rules and permission checks
- quote and scope before work
- documented visits with dated photos and care notes
- flowers/tributes, light tending, condition updates, and eligible cleaning in one family-facing service
- recurring seasonal care
- national-network potential, but only after local proof

Do not position Where They Rest as an engineering, conservation, monument-repair, or structural-restoration company.

## Stan — partnerships and compliant prospect research

### Goal
Build a small, high-quality referral and cemetery-relationship pipeline. Do **not** optimize for maximum cold-email volume.

### Initial prospect categories
1. cemetery operators and cemetery boards
2. monument/headstone companies
3. funeral homes and cremation providers
4. genealogical / cemetery-history organizations where a commercial relationship would genuinely fit
5. estate/elder-care professionals only after the memorial-sector list is worked

### Required prospect record
Before a Canadian commercial email is approved, retain:
- organization name
- recipient name/role if available
- exact email address
- the organization/recipient's own source URL where the email was conspicuously published
- date the address was checked
- whether any statement restricting unsolicited commercial email was present
- why the proposed message is relevant to that recipient's business role/functions
- status: research / draft ready / approved to send / sent / replied / opted out
- opt-out date if applicable

A third-party lead database by itself is **not** sufficient evidence for Canadian implied consent.

### CASL operating rule
For Canadian outreach, do not send unless there is a documented lawful basis for the message. When relying on conspicuous publication, the address must be conspicuously published by the recipient/account holder, not accompanied by a no-solicitation statement, and the message must be relevant to the recipient's business role or duties.

Every commercial message must include appropriate sender identification/contact information and a simple unsubscribe mechanism. Opt-outs must be suppressed immediately operationally and in all cases within the legal deadline.

### Sending control
**Draft-only by default. Do not autonomously send Where They Rest outreach emails. Owner approval is required before sending or activating an outbound sequence.**

This is intentional: first-wave messaging must be reviewed for fit, tone, cemetery sensitivity, and CASL evidence before launch.

## First-wave outreach angle

The first email to a cemetery or memorial-sector partner should be a **permission/fit question**, not a sales pitch.

Core idea:
- Where They Rest helps families who cannot visit in person.
- Services are family-authorized and limited to documented visits, approved tributes, photographs, light non-invasive care, and eligible cleaning.
- Ask what that operator allows and whether an independent provider can serve families under its rules.
- Make clear that Where They Rest wants to follow cemetery requirements, not work around them.

Do not ask for exclusivity. Do not imply partnership. Do not use inflated national-network claims.

## Kill / scale rule

After a genuine 90-day launch effort:

Scale if there are at least 10 unrelated paid customers, recurring uptake, credible partner/referral movement, and evidence that customer acquisition can be repeated.

If the business cannot attract 10 unrelated paying customers despite a working site, local coverage, consistent search/content work, and disciplined partnership outreach, stop investing major time and money and keep only the low-cost domain/brand option alive.
